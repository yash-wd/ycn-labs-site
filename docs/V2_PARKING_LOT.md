## NOT PARKED — explicit active safety and revenue work

- Truthful public claims and correction of misleading live-site statements, including the historical hotfix on Preview.
- A **verified** contact method and monitored real human recipient; backend data-loss/false-503 and concurrency defects if custom form remains live.
- Correct business identity, INR/India settings, actual legal/privacy notices and applicable professional review.
- Customer-data security and recoverability, authorized access, appropriate retention/erasure and breach response.
- Usable mobile site, accessibility basics, key responsive defects, real-device performance, approved logo/media rights.
- Green tests/build/CI, source-control safeguards, actual staging, commercial hosting permission, tested rollback and Owner's exact-SHA production deploy ruling.
- Customer outreach, honest evidence, delivery capacity and first commercial relationships.
- Existing owner instructions and material incident history as archived facts; the change is to routine *future workflow*, not to history.

## Evidence-based V2.0 decision form (June 2027)

Review: (1) real enquiry volume and missed-lead incidents, (2) outreach-to-proposal-to-win conversion, (3) delivery revenue and gross margin, (4) maintenance burden, (5) customer requests repeated across independent engagements, (6) cost and alternatives, (7) available technical/time capacity, (8) applicable privacy/security obligations.

For each row choose **Build now / Leave parked / Retire idea** with one paragraph and a measured reason. An outcome of **"build nothing; keep serving customers"** is valid. A future date by itself is never authorization to implement anything.
